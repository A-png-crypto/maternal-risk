ABOUT THE SYSTEM

How the prediction works

The system uses eight maternal health factors collected from the POLY CLINIC dataset to train an Artificial Neural Network.

The trained model analyzes the information entered for a new patient and predicts the patient's maternal health risk level.

Eight Health Factors

  • Age
  • Systolic Blood Pressure
  • Diastolic Blood Pressure
  • Blood Glucose
  • BMI
  • Heart Rate
  • Parity
  • Hemoglobin
